# Product scope (from the native app)

Expense-focused, single-user, local-first personal finance app importing MoneyLover history. The MoneyLover export is the source of truth for historical data; the unused native app is not a data source. The intended first release covers manual quick-add, balances, transaction search/filter, spending-by-category and monthly summaries, MoneyLover CSV import, and backup/export. Trends are deferred; budgets and recurring transactions are not planned. AI/LLM entry is out of scope for the Expo migration.

## Behavior to preserve

- Accounts have currencies; an Account's currency does not change once it has Transactions. Transfers are same-currency only. A separate editable display rate is used for combined cross-currency reporting.
- MoneyLover import maps signed amounts to Expenses/Income, preserves `Exclude Report` except for the lending-principal policy below, and seeds a category taxonomy. Mixed categories need user confirmation; high-confidence transfer pairs merge, while uncertain pairs enter the Review Queue (see `docs/adr/0001-moneylover-transfer-reconstruction.md`). Import must be safe against duplicates.
- Explicitly approved MoneyLover policy: `Loan` Expenses represent lending principal; `Debt Collection` Income represents repayments received; `Debt` Income represents borrowing; `Repayment` Expenses represent repayments made. All four are excluded from reports regardless of the CSV flag. Apply this to new imports and matching keyed imported Transactions on reimport, never manual entries. Preserve the original CSV and derive row identities from its original flags; amounts, Account balances, and Categories remain unchanged. Other category/direction flags retain their CSV meaning.
- Spending-by-category excludes Transfers, Income, and Transactions marked `excludeFromReport`; month summaries show income and expense separately.
- Lending and borrowing use shared saved people, separate balances by direction and Currency, aggregate repayments, overpaid credit and dated write-offs. Historical attribution links existing Transactions explicitly and may remain incomplete. See `docs/adr/0003-lending-linked-transactions.md`.
- Backup/restore preserves people, lending links, write-offs, Transactions, Line Items, tags, transfers, Accounts, currencies, and Categories. CSV export is a portability format, not a full-fidelity backup.
- Quick-add is amount-first, defaults to Expense and a configurable favorite Account, and defaults the date to today. Expense, Income, Transfer and Person share one form with inline Account and person selection; Person provides explicit lending, borrowing and repayment actions. Category search is recent-first and retains parent paths. Date, note and tags apply to every mode. Successful saves close directly without an Add another dialog; failures retain the draft. `excludeFromReport` is set on edit for ordinary Transactions; lending principal stays excluded. See `docs/adr/0004-unified-money-entry.md`.

This is intended behavior, not a claim that it works in Expo today. Native-platform plans such as SAF weekly exports and Android Auto Backup require a new Expo-specific design before implementation.
